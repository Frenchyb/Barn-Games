## Models (`models/`)
- Pig, Cow, Sheep, Horse: Farm Animals Pack by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimal.html), CC0 1.0. Converted from FBX to GLB with FBX2glTF. Pig and Sheep ship with only Idle and Jump clips, so they hop down the track.

## Sounds (`sounds/`)
From DJWoodZ/Animal-Sounds (https://github.com/DJWoodZ/Animal-Sounds), CC0 1.0, trimmed from these CC0 Freesound recordings:
- pig-oink.mp3: "Pig - Multiple Snorts 3" by JarredGibb (freesound.org/s/233182)
- cow-moo.mp3: "Cow moos" by josephsardin (freesound.org/s/177253)
- sheep-baa.mp3: "Flock of sheep pass by" by sandeepkurissery (freesound.org/s/245183)
- chicken-cluck.mp3: "Chicken Single Alarm Call" by Rudmer_Rotteveel (freesound.org/s/316920)

Rick picked the Day on the Farm set by ear on 2026-10-18. These CC0 Freesound recordings came trimmed and loudness-matched from Circuit-Stitch/AnimalSpin-Resources (https://github.com/Circuit-Stitch/AnimalSpin-Resources, records/asset-credits.csv), cut to at most 3.2 s, mono 48 kbps MP3. Each file is named after its AnimalSpin clip (for example duck_fs4); that CSV lists the Freesound ID and author of each.
- pig_fs1/2/4, cow_fs2/5/7/8/9, sheep_fs1/2/4/5 and lambs sheep_fs7/8, chicken_fs1/3/5, duck_fs4/5/6/7/9, frog_fs1/5, horse_fs2/3/4/5/6/8/9, goat_fs1 to goat_fs6.

Chickens and roosters:
- rooster-pd1 and hen-pd1, hen-pd2: "Small rooster crowing.ogg" and "Hen announcing shes lain an egg.ogg" by alys on Wikimedia Commons, public domain (via WindriderQc/AgentX).
- rooster-bsb1/2/3: BigSoundBank (Joseph Sardin) "Rooster" (0104) and "Song of a Rooster" (0474), BigSoundBank free licence: any use, no credit required (it is not formally CC0).

Chicken, goat, duck and frog models and all music are still built in code.

## Narrator (`../day-on-the-farm/vo/`)
Every line was spoken by the Kokoro TTS model (Apache 2.0, https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), voice bm_george (full-precision model, speed 0.95), and saved as MP3. Rick picked George.
